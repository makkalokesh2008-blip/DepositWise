💡 What We Learned

Centroids, Similarity, Inertia & Silhouette: K-Means partitions standardized customer vectors by iteratively assigning points to nearest centroids and minimizing within-cluster sum of squares (WCSS / Inertia). Evaluating candidate K values via the Elbow Method (inflection point) and Silhouette Analysis balances compactness with cluster separation using exclusively the six numerical banking attributes without target supervision.
