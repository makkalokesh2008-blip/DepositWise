Statistical Outliers vs. Invalid Data: In financial and banking data, extreme values (e.g. accounts with balance > ₹50,000 or clients contacted multiple times) represent genuine, highly valuable customer market segments rather than measurement errors. Truncating or deleting these records would introduce severe selection bias. They are retained and managed via robust scaling in downstream models.
