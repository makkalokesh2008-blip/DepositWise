Crucial Leakage Prevention Rule: Duration Omission

In bank marketing telemarketing campaigns, duration (call length in seconds) is only known during or after a contact conversation takes place. Prior to placing the telephone call, call duration is inherently 0 or unknown.

If duration were included as a training feature, classifiers achieve an unrealistic ~90% accuracy purely by memorizing that conversations exceeding 5 minutes almost invariably result in subscription. Such a model is practically useless for prospecting. DepositWise strictly excludes duration from all pre-call classification pipelines to ensure true forward-looking operational validity.

Encoding, Scaling & Leakage Prevention: Omission of the duration attribute is mandatory to prevent post-contact leakage in pre-call prospecting. Categorical features are converted via OneHotEncoder into 44 indicator columns without imposing arbitrary ordinality. Robust scaling parameters are fitted strictly on the 80% training partition, preventing data leakage into the 20% stratified validation set.
