💡 What We Learned

Agglomerative Clustering, Linkage & Dendrogram: Hierarchical agglomerative clustering constructs a bottom-up taxonomy of customer profiles without requiring pre-specified cluster counts. Linkage criteria (Ward's minimum variance, Complete, Average, Single) govern cluster merge geometry, while dendrogram distance thresholds establish clear, mathematically transparent partition cuts on standardized banking features.
