💡 What We Learned

Density, Epsilon, Min Samples, Core, Border & Noise: DBSCAN groups points based on spatial density rather than centroid proximity, eliminating the need to specify K. Points with at least min_samples within radius ε are designated core points, adjacent points become border points, and isolated records in low-density space are flagged as noise (-1). Automatic knee detection on the sorted k-nearest-neighbor distance curve provides a data-driven baseline for epsilon.
