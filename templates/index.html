Project Objective

Term deposits represent a critical liability portfolio and stable liquidity source for retail banking institutions. However, conventional cold-calling campaigns incur substantial operational costs, call-center fatigue, and low conversion rates (~11.7%). DepositWise develops an end-to-end data science and machine learning solution to accurately predict customer term-deposit subscription propensity before making contact, enabling data-driven priority outreach.

Data Leakage Prevention: The feature duration (call duration in seconds) is recorded during or after contact. Including it would inflate results while making the model unsuitable for pre-contact decisions, so it is excluded from all classification models.
