💡 What We Learned

Dimensionality Reduction, Principal Components & Explained Variance: PCA is an unsupervised dimensionality reduction technique, not a clustering algorithm. By computing eigenvectors of the standardized customer feature covariance matrix, PCA projects correlated numerical attributes into orthogonal, uncorrelated principal components. The scree plot and cumulative variance ratio quantify information retention, enabling effective 2D visualization and compression of customer financial profiles.
