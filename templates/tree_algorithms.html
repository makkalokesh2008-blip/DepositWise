💡 What We Learned

Decision Rules, Tree Splitting, Bagging, Boosting & Feature Importance: Single CART decision trees offer interpretable orthogonal decision boundaries but can overfit without pruning. Bagging ensembles (Random Forest, Extra Trees) reduce variance through bootstrap aggregation and random feature subsets. Sequential boosting algorithms (AdaBoost, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM) systematically minimize residual pseudo-errors, achieving superior discrimination (ROC-AUC > 0.795) driven primarily by customer age, balance, day of contact, and previous campaign success.
